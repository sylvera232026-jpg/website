Careers at Sylvera

We're building a professional, process-driven BPO and financial collections team in Chennai. Here's what that looks like day to day.

Working at Sylvera

Sylvera Corporation LLP provides BPO and financial collections support for banks, NBFCs and financial-service organizations. That work depends on people who can communicate clearly, follow structured processes, and handle customer interactions responsibly.

If that sounds like the kind of work you want to do, we'd like to hear from you — even if there isn't a specific open role listed below.

What we look for

  • Clear, professional communication

    Comfortable handling customer conversations with clarity and patience.

  • Comfort with structured processes

    Able to follow defined workflows and keep accurate records.

  • A responsible approach to collections and customer interactions

    Understands the difference between firm follow-up and pressure.

  • Attention to confidentiality and data handling

    Treats client and customer information with care.

Open roles

No specific openings listed right now

We don't have specific positions posted at the moment. We're generally interested in candidates for BPO operations, customer support and financial collections roles, and review applications on an ongoing basis.
